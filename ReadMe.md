## Instalysis ver Nw-csv (Integreted with UI of Notion)
#### Features
- **Totally Free** - Work with only PC browser and a notion account.
- **Simple Workflow** - 3 Steps to take a snapshot of your account.
- **Information Secure** - No certification or token is cached or provided to anywhere through Instalysis. You will grab data from www.instagram.com directly and import to your private Notion workspace through your PC.
